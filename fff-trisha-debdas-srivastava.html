FinTech Female Fridays: Meet Trisha Debdas Srivastava, Head of Operations at Avantos.ai

Trisha Debdas Srivastava Head of Operations · Avantos.ai LinkedIn →
Manvir Singh · Mar 6 · 6 min

In a world that constantly evolves, the paths we take in our careers and lives are rarely linear. Some journeys are shaped by ambition, while others are driven by passion, persistence, and the courage to pivot when the time is right. Trisha’s story is a testament to all of these forces. An inspiring example of resilience, creativity, and leadership. With a career spanning multiple industries and roles, Trisha’s journey is one that empowers women to break barriers, embrace challenges, and carve their own path to success.

A Diverse Career Built on Bold Pivots

Trisha’s career is nothing short of extraordinary. Starting as an architect designing villas in the Middle East, she ventured into technology as a software engineer, dove into management consulting, and later found her stride in the fast-paced worlds of Financial Services and FinTech. It’s a narrative that embodies adaptability and innovation.

Her ability to navigate and thrive in such varied sectors is a powerful reminder that we are not confined to a single identity or career trajectory. Trisha’s journey proves that we can and should embrace change, leverage our unique skills, and pursue the opportunities that speak to our core.

Currently, as the Head of Business Operations at Avantos.ai, Trisha is shaping the future of financial services with a next-generation platform that is revolutionizing onboarding and client servicing. With a strong focus on intelligent AI-driven service agents, an extendable user journey supporting data layer, and an intuitive user interface, Avantos is setting new standards in an ever-evolving industry.

But it’s not just the technology that makes Avantos so powerful, it’s the culture. Trisha leads in a collaborative environment that fosters bold thinking and encourages innovation. Her role demands versatility, constantly switching between the weeds of day-to-day operations and the broader strategic vision of the company.

For women considering careers in FinTech or any industry, Trisha offers a valuable piece of advice: embrace roles that challenge you to expand your perspective, take ownership, and communicate across disciplines. It’s this well-rounded experience, rooted in both leadership and execution, that can create a meaningful and impactful career.

The Power of Data and AI

Trisha’s fascination with technology isn’t limited to FinTech alone. She’s equally captivated by the transformative power of data and artificial intelligence. The potential to leverage data in new ways will shape the future, and Trisha is at the forefront of this movement, especially within AI-driven services.

“The ability to process data intelligently and apply it in innovative ways has the potential to revolutionize customer experiences across industries.”

It’s this forward-thinking mindset that will drive the next wave of technological advancements and redefine how businesses connect with their customers.

Lessons in Leadership and Empowerment

Trisha’s philosophy of leadership is one of authenticity, adaptability, and trust. She believes that true leadership is not about conforming to a specific mold but about carving one’s own path and empowering others along the way. She emphasizes that leadership is about switching between the details and the big picture, leading by example, and inspiring those around you to pursue their goals with confidence.

Her advice to women in leadership is simple yet powerful:

“Embrace your unique perspective and use it as a strength to inspire, influence, and drive change.”

By being genuine and confident, you create a legacy of trust and impact that not only elevates your career but also sets the stage for others to follow.

Building a Legacy of Impact

Looking back on her career, Trisha’s journey is a blueprint for resilience and growth. Her time at Goldman Sachs, her ventures in startups, and her leadership roles in FinTech have all contributed to her multifaceted approach to business. She understands that success is not defined by one role or title but by the lessons learned, the people impacted, and the changes made along the way.

Trisha’s commitment to innovation, to supporting others, and to challenging the status quo is something that all women can look up to and draw inspiration from. Her career is a reminder that no matter where you start or what obstacles you face, with courage, determination, and a willingness to embrace change, you can achieve extraordinary things.

Trisha’s journey is more than just a career — it’s a legacy of empowerment, innovation, and leadership. For women everywhere, Trisha’s story is a call to action: to break free from conventional expectations, embrace your unique strengths, and forge your own path in the world.

Through her work, her entrepreneurial ventures, and her leadership, Trisha is not just shaping industries — she is shaping the future, and in doing so, she’s empowering women everywhere to do the same.

What’s the best job decision you ever made?

The best job decision I ever made was choosing to work at Startups.

My experiences at Unqork and now at Avantos.ai have pushed me to explore beyond traditional boundaries, igniting creativity and resilience. Playing varied roles has given me a holistic understanding of the many levers required to build and scale a business.

This versatile exposure not only honed my professional skills but also inspired me to pursue my passion initiative: founding PeertoPlay.com, a referral based platform designed to empower parents in curating and enhancing their children’s social experiences during their formative years. Our app is currently available on both Android and iPhone, and I hope that parents love the experience, as we take the mundane away from shuttling between social channels to coordinate playdates or give children a broader exposure of peer network to choose from.

Working at startups has been instrumental in shaping my entrepreneurial mindset and drive.

What is the most important lesson you have learned from a mistake you’ve made in the past?

Mistakes are an important aspect of your life, count does not matter. I take mistakes and achievements with the same stride as they are all part of the learning process. Own your mistakes, move on, and try to implement the learnings from it.

Do you have any productivity hacks? What keeps you motivated? How do you maintain a work/life balance?

Productivity Hacks:

  1. Start with MITs (Most Important Tasks): Identify 2-3 key tasks each day that will have the most significant impact.
  2. Follow the 80/20 Rule (Pareto Principle): Focus on the 20% of tasks that yield 80% of results
  3. Make an effort to know your colleagues across different facets of the organization and don’t be afraid to ask for help!
  4. Seek regular feedback and reflect on your goals and pivot quickly.
  5. Clear your inbox before you go to bed

MOTIVATION: Family and a lot of travel keeps me motivated. Also being in a role where every day is a new day is thrilling and gives me the adrenaline rush!

BALANCE is overstated! It is not possible to have balance every day so gauge it on a holistic basis. One day the scale will be more tilted towards one side, the second day towards the other! Work at a place and with people who will help you strive for an overall balance and will support your life goals too!

Daily Diary

  • 7.00am - 8.00am: Get my son prepped for school (drop him to school on certain days). Eat breakfast with my husband.
  • 8.00am - 8.30am: Binge watch all news channels with a cup of tea in hand
  • 8.30am - 9.00am: Planning my day: Review my TO-DO’s, Meeting Calendar, and block some Focus time on the calendar
  • 9.00am - 6.00pm: Switch between client and internal meetings, work, discussions/brainstorming sessions with my colleagues and leadership
  • 6.00pm - 8.00pm: Team dinners or Family dinner!
  • 8.00pm - 9.00pm: Spend time with family or call up friends
  • 9.00pm - 10.30pm:. Finish off any pending work and spend some ME time (browsing social channels for food and travel, listen to music, read a book, and tuck my son to bed)
