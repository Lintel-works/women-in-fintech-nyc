FinTech Female Fridays: Meet The NYC FinTech Women Team

The NYC FinTech Women Team LinkedIn →
Elsie Russell · Nov 25 · 2 min

This Thanksgiving week, we are extra thankful for the 20+ volunteers who make NYC FinTech Women possible. To give thanks, we are featuring this incredible group for FinTech Female Fridays! Our team is brought together from all different FinTech verticles, traditional Finance, Legal and Tech, and from every career stage. Inspired and motivated by our mission of connecting, promoting and empowering women in their professional advancement in FinTech. Together, these amazing contributors put together our in-person and virtual events, networking opportunities, job postings, social media content, annual Inspiring FinTech Females List, FinTech Female Fridays blog, legal office hours--the list goes on! Our team is located not only in New York, but also across the US, from Miami, San Francisco, Seattle, and even in the UK.

Join our team! Apply to volunteer with us here or reach out to us at info@nycfintechwomen.com.
