The original chapter. Since 2018, NYC Fintech Women has been bringing together the women building, leading, and investing in financial services across New York City — one room at a time.

Our mission

Built for the women who are changing this city's financial future.

The New York chapter is the founding chapter of NYC Fintech Women and home to the largest concentration of fintech and financial services talent in the country. We run panels, mixers, roundtables, and the annual Inspiring Fintech Females ceremony — all designed to put women in the right rooms and give them the visibility they deserve.
