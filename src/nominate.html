Inspiring FinTech Females

Nominate a woman who belongs on this list.

Every year the Inspiring FinTech Females list recognizes women transforming fintech who are also advocates for women and gender equity. The list only works because people like you point us at women we would otherwise miss.

What makes a strong nomination

Specifics beat superlatives.

  • 1

    Tell us what she actually did

    A product she shipped, a team she built, a number that moved. Concrete work is what the judges can weigh.

  • 2

    Show how she lifts other women

    Mentoring, sponsorship, hiring, speaking, or quietly making room. This is half the award, not a footnote.

  • 3

    You can nominate anyone

    A colleague, a founder you admire, someone you have never met. She does not need to be a member, and you can nominate yourself.
