This commitment will be essential in attracting both capital and talent to your company. Once you take the leap, be focused on the top priorities that will move the business forward. Surround yourself with great advisers. You will be amazed at the willingness of people to help. I of course scrambled to prove her right, my own self-image was boosted, and others began to see me as a rising talent.
