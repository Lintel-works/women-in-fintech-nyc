As" blocks: - type: image src: "images/fff-devika-tampi-1.jpg" alt: "Devika Tampi" - type: qa q: "Can you talk about your career path and what led you to pivot from Program Director at Empire Startups to Senior Manager at Innovation Ecosystem at [Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/)?" a: "After starting my career as a lawyer in Australia, I went back to college to study social impact. AI will continue to drive personalization forward, and in the wealth management space, FinTechs will continue to open up new asset classes, democratizing investing." - type: qa q: "What has been the biggest challenge in your career thus far and how have you overcome it?" a: "I think making the decision to leave the legal field. At the time, I wasn't entirely sure what I wanted to do next, but knew that while I continued to work at the firm, I was never going to figure it out. I left without a new job lined up, and worked in a bar for 6 months at night to pay rent while doing multiple internships during the day to experience different kinds of jobs. And it paid off! It was a challenging time, but looking back, I trusted my gut, hustled and leaned on my support network. Which I've done many times since." - type: qa q: "What is one piece of advice for women looking to enter the FinTech space?" a: "Build a community. And do not be afraid to let people know what you're looking for.
