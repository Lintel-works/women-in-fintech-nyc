However, I started my career in tech and finance and evolved into building institutional operational and regulatory infrastructure to help businesses scale. Graduating from Cornell during the tech boom, my first "real job" was Project Manager at Charles River Consultants - not a traditional path for an English major. I moved on to Credit Suisse and spent six years on the trading floor in Emerging Markets Fixed Income in New York City and London. Upon returning to New York from London, I helped a college friend start his FinTech consulting firm MPI Professionals. From there I took on the Chief Operating Officer/Chief Compliance Officer (COO/CCO) role at New York Private Bank & Trust where I launched their Wealth Management division. My interest in alternative investments drove my decision to join Alternative Investment Management, an adviser investing in hedge and private equity funds, also as COO/CCO. Then, while serving as an outsourced COO/CCO for alternative investment vehicles, I helped a family office build [6th Avenue Capital](https://6thavenuecapital.com/). When they asked me join the company full-time as CEO, it brought me full circle back to the small business world, combining finance and technology.
