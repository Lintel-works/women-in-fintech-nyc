Our mission

This community exists because the industry wasn't built for us. We're changing it from within.

The number of women leading, building, and investing in financial services has grown — but the structural barriers, missing connections, and rooms women still aren't in haven't disappeared on their own. NYC Fintech Women exists because women in this industry deserve a community built specifically for them — not an afterthought or a diversity initiative. A substantive network where careers get accelerated and ambitions get taken seriously.

If you're ready to build your real career in a real community with likeminded women, this is where you start.
