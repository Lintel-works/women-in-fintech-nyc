04 — Photography

Member portrait treatment

Member portraits use a brand-color duotone overlay by default. Preferred treatment for IFF and member grids: soft plum (#8b4a7a at 65% opacity) over a desaturated photo. On hover (web), the overlay lifts to reveal the full-color photo. Alternate with teal overlays so grids do not read as all pink.
